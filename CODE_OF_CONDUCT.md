# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment. Be constructive, assume good intent, welcome different levels of experience, and focus criticism on ideas and changes rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using the contact information on the owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not permitted.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
